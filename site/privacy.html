Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

What we collect

We collect your email address when you sign in with Google. This is the only personal information AudioProctor stores.

What we do not collect

Students using AudioProctor are anonymous to the system. We do not collect student names, student IDs, ASN numbers, device identifiers, IP addresses, or any information that could identify a student. No student data is written to our database at any point during a session.

How teacher data is used

Your email address is used solely to associate your uploaded audio files with your account. It is not shared with third parties, not used for marketing, and not sold.

Data storage

Audio files and account data are stored with Supabase on infrastructure located in North America. All data in transit is encrypted via TLS. Audio files are stored in private buckets and are never publicly accessible.

Data retention

Audio files expire and are deleted automatically based on the expiry window set at upload. You may delete your files at any time from the teacher dashboard. To delete your account entirely, contact us at hello@audioproctor.com.

Third-party services

AudioProctor uses Google OAuth for authentication. Google's privacy policy governs that interaction. We do not use third-party analytics services or advertising networks.

FOIP

AudioProctor does not trigger a Privacy Impact Assessment under Alberta's Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act. The only personal data processed — teacher email addresses — is already governed by existing Google Workspace agreements at participating school divisions. No student personal information is collected or stored.
