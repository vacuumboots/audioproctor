AudioProctor
Accessible assessment audio
for Alberta classrooms

Provincial testing tools don't support audio accommodations. AudioProctor fills that gap with a simple, locked-down audio player that teachers control and students can't bypass.

The problem today

Students requiring audio accommodations during Vretta assessments are served by a patchwork of shared generic accounts, MP3 files on Google Drive, and manual setup — creating extra administrative burden, privacy concerns, and no reliable way to prevent students from wandering outside the test environment.

The solution

A teacher uploads an MP3 to the AudioProctor dashboard and generates a session link and one-time PIN. A managed Chromebook extension opens the audio in a locked browser overlay — no new tabs, no escape without the teacher's PIN. Clean, simple, and purpose-built for assessment day.

Google Sign-In

Deployed silently via Google Admin to any OU. Students never install anything manually.
